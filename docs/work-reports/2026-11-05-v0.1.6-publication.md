## Scope

Physical keyboard/mouse/gamepad Binding settings and per-device diagnostics, runtime configuration, version-1 persistence, one-input capture, the existing Action composition and pointer ownership, and paired guides/lab. Legacy keyboard-only configurations and explicit gamepad bindings remain supported. Chords, sequences, pattern recognition, analog-axis capture and a standard key-configuration widget remain deferred.

## Known limits

Physical devices/gamepad reconnects, pen/vendor buttons, actual touch/OS variations, Safari/Firefox, hardware GPU and assistive technologies remain unverified. OS/browser-reserved gestures may remain unavailable. Earlier desktop WebGL/frozen-clock checks observed intermittent browser responsiveness; their unchanged retries succeeded, and the cause remains unestablished. The previous local generated-output anomaly and its successful clean/CI rechecks are retained in the implementation report. No new hardware/browser support is claimed by this release.
